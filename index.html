About Company

Credible performance, rising stage by stage.

BAPTA engineering was originally founded in Arusha in 2025 as a Building and Civil Engineering Contractors and was registered by the national Contractor Registration Board (CRB) in Class Five.

Since then, it has been involved mainly in building maintenance, rehabilitations, new construction and occasionally road repairs and supply of building materials and as a result of its credible performance it has been rising stage by stage until now is in Class Four for Buildings and Class V for Civil Works. BAPTA is now also venturing into property development business.

Personnel

Experienced people, strong project management

BAPTA’s services are provided by a highly experienced and educated, in-house staff of licensed professionals, project managers, and technicians supplemented by a consortium of highly qualified professional consultants from within and outside of the country.

Consortium consultants include Architects Engineers, Quantity Surveyors, Human Resource, Specialists, and a Matrix of Technicians with professional qualifications, experience, and reputation, combined with their versatility and ability to provide service in a timely manner.

This unique blending of resources through the application of strong project management skills is strength for the company’s success.

Company Experience

An unparalleled reputation for a wide range of services

As a major national construction and building contractors, Bapta Engineering has built an unparalleled reputation for a wide range of services. From contract maintenance work to new construction projects, road works, bridges, office renovations to commercial renovations; Bapta Engineering has been doing these works for the past seven years now through many construction companies that have been gaining experience and eventually obtaining the company registration, with that experience we create an environment that perfectly matches our client’s vision.

We offer a complete construction package to our clients. All sites are managed by projects from start to finish and a designated site manager oversees the work of our technicians. Our comprehensive approach ensures that every project from bathroom installations to complete renovations and structural extensions is completed on time and on budget and to the highest standards.

As serious builders, we believe that a tidy workplace is as important as the final finish and we strive to keep our workplace tidy and safe.
